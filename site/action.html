    What we’re asking for

    Our demands

    Every OneW campaign comes back to the same four words: call, investigate, reunify, restore.

    Start with a call

    1. Call your Congressperson

      The Deal of 1836 was an Act of Congress. Only Congress can revisit it. Make sure your representative knows the name Toledo.

    2. Demand investigations

      Open the records of the Frostbitten Convention, the Deal, and every records request Lansing has denied.

    3. Demand Reunification

      Restore the northeastern lands of Greater Wisconsin to the state they border, share a time zone with, and cheer the same football team as.

    4. Demand Restitution

      A full accounting of the copper, iron and lumber taken from Occupied Northeast Wisconsin since 1837.

    Call Congress

    Make the call

    It takes three minutes. Staffers tally every call.

    1. 1

      Find your representative

      Look up your U.S. Representative and their office phone number by ZIP code.

    2. 2

      Use the script

      Be polite and brief. Staff are people too, even in Lansing.

    3. 3

      Tell us how it went

      Did they laugh? Did they ask what Toledo has to do with it? Every response is data.

    Hello, my name is [your name] and I’m a constituent from [your town].

    I’m calling to ask the Representative to support a full congressional investigation into the Act of June 15, 1836, which transferred the northeastern lands of Greater Wisconsin to Michigan as compensation for Toledo.

    I believe the people of Occupied Northeast Wisconsin deserve the truth, and Reunification. Thank you for your time.

    Then fill in the brackets.
