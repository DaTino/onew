Who we are

One Wisconsin. Whole again.

OneW is a nonpartisan advocacy organization working for the peaceful Reunification of Greater Wisconsin, from the Mississippi to the St. Marys River.

Our mission

To document the Occupation of Northeast Wisconsin, expose the coverup that has kept it hidden for nearly two centuries, and build the movement that will bring it home.

Our vision

A Wisconsin made whole: one state, one people, one time zone (eventually), united on both shores of Green Bay and along the full southern coast of Lake Superior.

Our story

It started with a slideshow

In 2019, a twelve-slide presentation titled Occupied Northeast Wisconsin: An American Travesty laid out the evidence: a surveyor’s error, a war over Toledo, a deal with “President” Jackson, and a peninsula handed to Michigan as a consolation prize.

The audience had questions. Why had no one heard of this? Why were the records so hard to find? Why does Michigan keep saying no?

OneW was founded to answer them. Our name is our argument: Occupied NorthEast Wisconsin is, and will one day again be, One Wisconsin.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Isn’t the Upper Peninsula part of Michigan?

Legally, since 1837. Geographically, we invite you to look at a map. The peninsula shares a long land border with Wisconsin. Its connection to Michigan is a five-mile bridge built in 1957.

What about the Yoopers?

They are Wisconsinites who have been told otherwise for 189 years. We say that with love. Reunification is something we want to do with the people of the Northeast, not to them.

Do you want a war?

Absolutely not. The last war over this border was about Toledo, and Wisconsin lost it without even being there. OneW is committed to peaceful, legal Reunification through Congress.

What happens to Michigan?

Michigan will remain a proud, mitten-shaped state. It will simply stop holding onto something that was never its own.

Is OneW a real organization?

OneW is a work of satire. The history on this site is real; the outrage is performative. No money is collected and no personal information is stored anywhere but your own browser.
